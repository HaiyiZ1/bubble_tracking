There are 3 cases (case1, case2 and case3), each case have 4 airs (air1, air2, air3 and air4).

The mm-to-pixel conversion, or reversed pixel-to-mm conversion for 4 airs belongs to the same case are the same.

For original videos (before downscale 0.5):
- For all 4 airs of case1: 100 mm = 2335 pixel
- For all 4 airs of case2: 100 mm = 2300 pixel
- For all 4 airs of case3: 100 mm = 2420 pixel

For downscaled videos (after downscale 0.5):
- For all 4 airs of case1: 200 mm = 2335 pixel
- For all 4 airs of case2: 200 mm = 2300 pixel
- For all 4 airs of case3: 200 mm = 2420 pixel

The downscaled videos are the videos we commonly used for data processing in this project. Always be alert on check whether it is downscaled, and usually it is downscaled 0.5 in most situations in this project.
